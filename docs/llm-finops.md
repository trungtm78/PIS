## What an attempt costs (BR-LLM-04)

`noncached = input_total − cached_input` when the price counts the cached tokens in the input (`INCLUDED_IN_INPUT`), and `input_total` when it counts them apart (`SEPARATE`); `cost = noncached × input_rate + cached × cached_rate + output_total × output_rate + tool_fee` with the rates per million tokens; `cost_vnd = round_half_up(cost × fx_rate_to_vnd, 0)`. Reasoning tokens are in `output_total` when the price says so and are never added again. All of it is exact: rates are decimal strings of up to eight places, held as integers (`BigInt`), never as floating point. 120,000 input of which 100,000 cached, and 10,000 output, at 2.00, 0.50 and 8.00 USD per million and 25,400 dong to the dollar, cost 0.17 USD and 4,318 VND; the same attempt reported as 20,000 + 100,000 under `SEPARATE` costs the same. The attempt keeps the currency, the cost in it, the exchange rate and the cost in dong (`llm_attempt`).

## Budgets and counters (BR-LLM-05)

A budget is a limit for a scope (`TENANT`, `UNIT`, `PROJECT`, `USER`) and a period (month, quarter, year); its counter for the period (made when it is first used) holds `settled_vnd` and `reserved_open_vnd`, and `available = limit − settled − reserved_open`. The database refuses `reserved_open > limit`. A scope has one ACTIVE budget for a period (`409 BUDGET_SCOPE_DUPLICATE`; the baseline index let a tenant budget be made twice, V1142 fixes it), and a unit, project or person cannot have more than the tenant for the same period, nor the tenant less than one of them (`422 CHILD_EXCEEDS_PARENT`).

- **Reserve** (`LlmFinopsStore.reserve`, used by the gateway, ST-LLM-005). A request is held against every budget of the scopes it belongs to, whatever the period (a month and a year of one scope are two ceilings over it; a scope without a budget does not limit it). The counters are locked one at a time in a fixed order (tenant, unit, project, user; by reference and id inside a kind), so two requests that need the same counters wait in line and never for each other. The estimate fits when it is at most what is left at every scope; the first scope, in lock order, that cannot take it is named. A budget that stops (`hard_stop`) gives `DENIED` (the gateway answers `429 BUDGET_EXCEEDED` naming the scope); a budget that does not gives `APPROVAL_REQUIRED` (a decision of a person raises the limit instead of the provider being called). A blocked scope refuses everything (`BLOCKED`). 20 simultaneous 50,000 jobs against a unit with 80,000 left hold one and refuse 19, with no deadlock; requests that name the same three scopes in opposite orders finish too.
A request is held once (`budget_reservation_request_uq`): a repeat of the admission, also at the same moment, is answered with the hold already made, and a hold that was released is not made again (`409 RESERVATION_NOT_ACTIVE`).
- **Settle** (`settle`), once for each provider request: the cost comes from the price version named, `settled += actual`, `reserved_open −= reserved`, and what was not used is released. The reservation is locked first, then the attempt of that `provider_request_id` is looked for, so a second delivery of the same callback, also at the same moment, waits and gets the first answer (`replayed: true`); the attempt row is unique by `provider_request_id` and keeps the answer it got (what it released, whether it went over, whether it crossed the soft line), so that a repeat gets the same answer for as long as the attempt exists; a provider request id that belongs to another request is `409 IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`. A request that needed a retry or a fallback is settled attempt by attempt: an attempt that is followed by another (`final: false`) takes only its own cost out of the hold, which stays open; the last one (`final`, the default) releases what is left. The usage of an attempt that arrives after the request ended (a primary that timed out and is reconciled once a fallback had answered) is real cost with no hold left: it is charged to the counters as it is, nothing is released a second time, and if what is settled and what is still held no longer fit in the limit the scope is blocked. An actual cost above the reservation is an exception: the numbers stand (the cost is real), the exception is counted, the reservation is `EXCEPTION` and the scope is blocked for the next requests.
- **Soft threshold** (BR-LLM-07). The settlement that takes a counter from below the soft line (default 80 percent of the limit) to or above it leaves one `llm.budget.threshold_reached.v1`, once for each counter, that is for each period of each budget (the event key is the counter).
- **No usage** (BR-LLM-06). A request that timed out or came back with no usage is `PENDING_RECONCILE` and keeps its hold: a missing figure is never taken as zero dong. A late callback settles it (once). The job `llm-reservation-expiry` (every 5 minutes) does the same for a reservation still `RESERVED` past its end, with the note `TIMEOUT_NO_USAGE`, and reports the age of the oldest open one as `llm_budget_reservation_age_seconds`. A reservation that will never be settled is released on a decision with a reason (`release`).

## Price versions (BR-LLM-13)

`registerPricingVersion` registers a version for a model of the tenant: it must name the model and the id it has (`422 VALIDATION_FAILED` on `model_ref_id`), carry its source (`422 SOURCE_URL_REQUIRED`), rates that are not negative and an exchange rate above zero. It must begin after every version the model has, and then ends the one in force where it begins; one that begins at or before a version the model has is `409 PRICING_RANGE_OVERLAP`. The database holds the same (one version in force for a model, one version for a moment). The price in force at a moment is `priceAt`. A new version is listed as **not verified**: the figure is the one the declarer gave.

## Spend and screens

`getAiSpendSummary` answers the budget, the counters by period and, for the period of today (Vietnam time), the tokens of the attempts settled against it (input, cached, uncached, output) and their cost, with the number of reservations that wait for the reconciliation. **LLM-07** lists the budgets with settled, held and left (and the share used), the quota, whether the budget stops or asks for an approval, a block after an exception, and a link to LLM-09; **LLM-08** the price versions with the source and the verified state; **LLM-09** the counter of a budget, the tokens and the reservations: those that wait for the reconciliation in a list of their own, said to be still held, and the recent ones after them, each read a page of 50 at a time with a button for the next (the list grows with every request), and a failure to read a list is told beside it without hiding the budget. Each has a form to create a budget or register a price, and the standard states (an empty list still offers to create the first).

## Assumptions and limits

- **Verification of a price by a second person** (BR-LLM-13) has no operation in the contract and `pricing_version` keeps neither who declared it nor when: the audit row of the registration names the actor, and a price is shown as not verified. A `verifyPricingVersion` step is to be added with the permission matrix of the roles; until then nothing treats an unverified price differently.
- **The approval task** of a budget that does not stop is not created: `approval_task` belongs to a workflow instance (the ADM approval workflow), so the store answers `APPROVAL_REQUIRED` and the gateway (ST-LLM-005) records the request as waiting; creating the task is left to the workflow.
- **Quotas** (`max_requests_per_day`, `max_concurrent`) are kept and shown; their enforcement is a check of the gateway at admission (F1 step 3, ST-LLM-005).
- **Reconciliation of invoices** (`reconcileAiInvoice`, `post_budgets_id_reconcile`) is not in this story. LLM-09 shows the reconciliation state of the reservations; the detail of each attempt (model, route, invoice) comes with the gateway.
- The fallback cap of an estimate is a parameter of `estimateVnd`; the units of the exchange rate source (`fx_source`) are free text.
- The permissions `llm.finops` and `budget_reservation.read` are tenant-wide: who holds them on a unit only is refused. The price form chooses the model from the catalogue (`listAiModels`, `llm.provider.read`), so a role that registers prices needs that right as well; without it the form says so and sends nothing.
