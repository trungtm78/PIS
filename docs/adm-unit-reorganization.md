# Reorganisation of organisation units (ST-ADM-015, ADM 3.2)

A merge, split, dissolution or rename of units: the members, the grants and the projects of the old units go to the successor, the old units end with their names and history kept. It is a saga of idempotent steps carried out by a job; there is no Temporal in the repository, so the saga keeps its own state in the database (`unit_reorganization`, `unit_reorganization_step`).

## Commands

* `proposeUnitReorganization` (`POST /unit-reorganizations`, 201, `iam.unit.reorganize` on the **whole tenant**). Judged in this order, under the lock of the unit tree:
  1. the shape of the kind (kernel `validateReorganization`, 422 with every defect by field): **MERGE** two or more old units into one successor; **SPLIT** one old unit into two or more existing successors; **DISSOLVE** one or more old units into one successor that takes over; **RENAME** one old unit into one successor (usually a new unit). A successor is an existing unit or a new unit (`new_unit`, only when it is the sole successor: its id does not exist yet, so a mapping can never name it). A successor is never an old unit. `TRANSFER` is in the contract but is the move of a unit (`moveOrganizationUnit`): 422 `kind`. The effective date is a real date, at most a year ahead; a past date is allowed (the legal text may be older);
  2. the old units exist in the tenant (404 otherwise), are Active (422), do not begin after the effective date (422 `UNIT_VALIDITY_INVALID` `effective_from`), have no active child unit that is not itself reorganised (422 `UNIT_VALIDITY_INVALID` `predecessor_unit_ids` with `stranded_child_unit_ids`), and are not named by another reorganisation that is not over (409 `REORG_STATE_INVALID`); the existing successors are Active or Planned; a new unit's parent exists;
  3. the mapping names only members and projects the old units hold, and **nothing is left behind**: every live member (Active, Suspended, Invited) and every project that is not CLOSED of the old units has a successor, the only one when there is one, otherwise its own entry or `default_successor_unit_id`. Otherwise 422 `REORG_MAPPING_INCOMPLETE` with `missing_membership_ids`, `missing_project_ids` and `errors[{field, id}]`, and **nothing is stored** (the criterion says the reorganisation "stays Draft": with no operation to edit a draft, a refused proposal leaves no draft behind).
  The proposal is stored PendingApproval (the table keeps it in `mapping_json`; it never changes).
* `decideUnitReorganization` (`POST /unit-reorganizations/{id}/decision`, **202**, `If-Match`, `iam.unit.reorganize.approve` on the whole tenant): by someone **other than the proposer** (403 `SELF_APPROVAL_DENIED`, judged on the membership, and the table forbids the same). `approve`: PendingApproval, or Failed (decided again), becomes **Applying** and `iam.org_unit.reorganization_approved.v1` is written; `return`: PendingApproval becomes Draft; `reject`: becomes Cancelled. Any other state is 409 `REORG_STATE_INVALID`; a stale version is 409 `VERSION_CONFLICT`. Nothing is moved by the decision.
* Reads (CR-ADM-16, new in `openapi.json`): `get_resources_unit_reorganization` (`status`) and `get_resources_unit_reorganization_id`, permission `iam.unit.reorganize.read` on the whole tenant: the proposals newest first with the old and new units by the names they have now, the counts of the mapping, who proposed and approved, the decision, the result and, by id, the steps done (the timeline of ADM-16).

## The saga (job `unit-reorganization`, every minute)

For each reorganisation that is Applying and whose **effective date (Vietnam date) has come** (one dated later waits), the job takes steps, each in a transaction of its own (at most 200 per reorganisation per pass), in this order; a step is found by what is left to do, so a step done is never found again, and each leaves a row `reorg:{id}:{step}:{item}` and an audit row:

1. `create-successor`: the new successor unit is created (valid from the effective date);
2. `activate-successor`: a successor that was Planned becomes Active;
3. `succession:{old}`: the rows of `organization_unit_succession` (old to each successor);
4. `membership:{id}`: each live member (Active, Suspended or Invited) of an old unit moves (`organization_id`) to its successor (kernel `successorOf`); one with no successor stops the saga (422 `REORG_MAPPING_INCOMPLETE`, someone joined after the proposal);
5. `grant:{id}`: each active grant over an old unit (unit subtree or only the unit) is made again over the successor (the sole one, else the default, else all) with `reorganization_id`, and the old grant **Expires** (`effective_until` now); a holder who already has the same role over the same scope keeps what they have; the scope version of the holder is raised;
6. `project:{id}`: each project that is not CLOSED goes to its successor through the **port** `ReorganizationProjectPort` with the key `reorg:{id}:project:{project}` (see below);
7. `retire:{old}`: the old unit becomes **Superseded** (merge, split, rename) or **Dissolved** (dissolve), `valid_until` = the effective date, `successor_id` = the default or only successor; its name is not touched;
8. the last step: the reorganisation is **Applied** (`applied_at`, `result_summary` with the counts), `iam.org_unit.reorganization_applied.v1` once and one bulk `iam.access.changed.v1` with the members whose access changed.

A step that fails rolls back; the job marks the reorganisation **Failed** in a transaction of its own, with `result_summary.failed = {step, code}`, the audit row `org_unit.reorganization_failed` and the gauge `unit_reorganization_failed_total` (alert `ADM_UNIT_REORGANIZATION_FAILED`). The steps done stay done. After the cause is mended a person other than the proposer **decides again** (`approve`) and the saga goes on from the step that failed. A reorganisation that is Applied or Cancelled is final (guard trigger of V1308).

## The project port (XM-ADM-02)

Moving a project belongs to PRJ (`requestProjectTransfer` needs a request, a decision and an apply, and refuses a service actor). Until PRJ accepts a transfer from the saga, the port bound in production is `UnboundProjectTransferPort`: it refuses with 503 `PROJECT_TRANSFER_UNAVAILABLE`, so **a unit that holds projects cannot be reorganised on a guess**; the saga stops at the first project and is carried on when it is decided again after PRJ is bound (`reorganizationProjects` of the worker dependencies). The tests bind a recording double. The step does not look again at who owns the project: the port answers for the transfer.

## ADM-16

`ReorganizationScreen`: the list of proposals (kind, old units, successors, date, basis, state) and, for the one opened, the timeline of what the saga has done (successors, members, grants, projects, old units; the step that failed and its code; the figures once applied); "decide" where the server says the viewer may, with a reason (approve, return, reject; a failed saga can be approved again or rejected). The wizard of four steps: **choose** the kind, the old units, the successors (or a new unit) with the date and the legal basis, **map** (the default successor of a split), **preview** (the units, the date, how many active members will move) and **submit**. It does not go on past a step that is not whole; a refusal is said in words, with how many members and projects have no successor.
