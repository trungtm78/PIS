# Archive handover, disposal proposals and retention rule management (ST-DOC-017)

Module document: `Design/MACHVON_Design/01_Documents/05_modules/DOC.md` (F11, BR-DOC-22, BR-DOC-23, DOC-17, DOC-18, UAT-AC421, UAT-AC422). Migration `V1229__doc1_archive_disposal_retention.sql`. Operations: `handoverDossierToArchive`, `proposeDossierDisposal`, `decideDossierDisposal`, `createDocumentRetentionRule`, `publishDocumentRetentionRule`, `confirmDocumentRetentionRuleLegalBasis`; reads `listDossiers`, `getDossier`, `listDocumentRetentionRules` (extended). Job `doc-handover-due`.

## The rule

A dossier that is closed goes to the archive; one whose retention is over can be proposed for disposal, and a council decides it. Nothing is deleted by DOC: a disposal only marks the dossier DISPOSED and tells the archive process of the tenant (`doc.dossier_disposed.v1`). The retention itself is data: a **rule** is drafted by one person, published by another and confirmed in law by a third.

- **Handover (`handoverDossierToArchive`, `doc.dossier.archive`, `If-Match`).** CLOSED to HANDED_OVER to ARCHIVED in one command (HANDED_OVER is the step the archive passes through, so the version moves twice). The body names the reference the archive gave (3 to 200 characters) and the moment of the handover, which is not in the future and not before the closing. The outgoing documents filed in the dossier become ARCHIVED, each with a line `ARCHIVED` in its timeline; incoming letters and documents that are not FILED any more are left as they are. A dossier that is not CLOSED is 422 `DOSSIER_NOT_CLOSED`.
- **Proposal (`proposeDossierDisposal`, `doc.dossier.archive`).** From CLOSED, HANDED_OVER or ARCHIVED, with a reason of 10 to 2000 characters. The refusals come in this order: the state (422 `DOSSIER_NOT_CLOSED`), a retention that is provisional (422 `RETENTION_PROVISIONAL`), a legal hold on the dossier, its unit, its project or contract, or the whole tenant (409 `LEGAL_HOLD_ACTIVE`, also when the retention is over), a retention that has not ended (422 `RETENTION_NOT_EXPIRED`). A dossier whose disposal is proposed or decided is 409 `STAGE_TRANSITION_DENIED`. Who proposed, when, why and from which state are kept on the dossier.
- **Decision (`decideDossierDisposal`, `doc.dossier.dispose`).** Only for a dossier in DISPOSAL_PROPOSED, and not by the proposer (403 `SELF_APPROVAL_DENIED`: the proposer may hold the right to decide, and is refused). `APPROVE` needs `council_decision_document_id`, a document of the tenant that was issued or received (422 `DECISION_DOCUMENT_REQUIRED` otherwise), and a hold put on the dossier after the proposal still stops it (409 `LEGAL_HOLD_ACTIVE`): the dossier is DISPOSED, final, and keeps who proposed, who decided, when and the document. `REJECT` takes no document and sends the dossier back to the state it was proposed from, clearing the proposal (the audit row and the event keep both people and what the decider wrote).
- **Retention rules.** `createDocumentRetentionRule` drafts a rule (`doc.retention.manage`): the rule set, a category (`^[A-Z][A-Z0-9_]{1,59}$`), the class (the years are those of the class: PERMANENT has none, Y70, Y20, Y10 and Y5 their own; a value that disagrees is 422), the start basis, the months to the handover (0 to 240, 12 by default), the precedence (1 to 1000, 100 by default), the legal basis (422 `LEGAL_BASIS_REQUIRED` when empty) and the period (the end is the first day the rule no longer applies). A period that overlaps a **published** rule of the same set and category, or that starts the same day as any rule of them, is 409 `RETENTION_RANGE_OVERLAP`. `publishDocumentRetentionRule` (`doc.retention.approve`) is by someone other than the drafter (403 `SELF_APPROVAL_DENIED`), only for a DRAFT (409 `RETENTION_STATE_INVALID`), and checks the overlap again against the published ones (two drafts may overlap each other; the second cannot be published once the first is). `confirmDocumentRetentionRuleLegalBasis` (`doc.template.confirm_legal`) is only for a published rule that waits, with a reference of 3 to 50 characters (422 `CONFIRMATION_REF_REQUIRED`) and an evidence document of the tenant, by someone who neither drafted nor published it. The three rights are held over the whole tenant: a grant on a branch of the tree is 403 `SCOPE_DENIED`, as for the templates.
- **The rule of a dossier.** Closing takes the rule in force on the day (UAT-AC422): a dossier closed on the last day of R-old keeps R-old and one closed the day R-new starts takes R-new, and what closing wrote does not change when a rule is published later. A dossier closed while no rule of its category is confirmed is provisional and keeps that when a rule is confirmed later; a dossier closed after the confirmation takes the confirmed rule (UAT-AC421).
- **The hold indicator.** `listDossiers` and `getDossier` carry `legal_hold_active` and `disposal_blocker` (the first thing that stops a disposal now), and `actions` with `handover`, `proposeDisposal` and `decideDisposal` for what the caller may do now. The proposer is not offered the decision.
- **The trail.** Audit rows `dossier.archived|disposal_proposed|disposed|disposal_rejected` and `document_retention_rule.draft|publish|confirm_legal`, with the events `doc.dossier_archived.v1`, `doc.dossier_disposal_proposed.v1`, `doc.dossier_disposed.v1`, `doc.dossier_disposal_rejected.v1`, `doc.retention_rule_drafted.v1`, `doc.retention_rule_published.v1`, `doc.retention_rule_confirmed.v1`. The audit rows and events carry ids and the facts of the step, never the title of a dossier.

## The job `doc-handover-due`

Hourly. For a CLOSED dossier whose `handover_due_on` is 30, 7 or 1 days away (a day that has passed counts as the last stage), the memberships that may hand a dossier over in its unit are told by `doc.handover_due.v1` (resource type `doc.dossier`, deep link `/doc/doc-17?id=...`), once for each stage: the stage is kept in `handover_reminded_days` (30, 7 or 1), so running the job again announces nothing twice. The version of the dossier does not move, and a dossier that was handed over is left alone. Metric `doc_handover_due_total`.

## The database

`V1229` adds who handed over, the proposal (who, when, why, from which state), the decision (who, when, the comment) and the stage of the reminder, with checks that say the same as the application: a handed-over dossier has its reference and its time; a proposal is complete; a decider is not the proposer (`dossier_disposal_sod_chk`); a reason is 10 to 2000 characters. The trigger `guard_dossier` now lets a rejected proposal go back to the state it was proposed from, refuses a provisional dossier for a proposal, keeps what the handover wrote, and makes a disposed dossier final. For the rules, the trigger `guard_doc_retention_rule` keeps the content of a rule, moves it only DRAFT to PUBLISHED (with the approver and the time) to SUPERSEDED, writes the approval and the confirmation once, refuses a confirmer who drafted or published, and nothing is deleted or truncated by the application role (the data owner is exempt, as for the other governed tables).

## The screens

`DossiersScreen` (DOC-17): the dossiers with their state, retention (provisional, the end, over), hold indicator, what stops a disposal, and the archive reference; a filter by state; the steps the server offers (hand over, propose, decide) as forms with the checks of the contract before they are sent. `RetentionRulesScreen` (DOC-18): the rules with their period, state, confirmation and the three people; publish (for an approver other than the drafter), confirm (for a confirmer who is neither) and a form to draft a rule whose years follow its class. Both show the seven standard states and take no step in a read-only session.

## What is not done

- The format of the receipt of the archive depends on the tenant: the reference is a free text of 3 to 200 characters.
- Rules are not edited or retired (only drafted, published and confirmed); a published rule ends where the next one starts, and a rule of the platform packs is not part of this story.
- Rejecting a disposal returns the dossier to the state it was proposed from (CLOSED, HANDED_OVER or ARCHIVED), not always to ARCHIVED as the table of states reads, because a dossier proposed from CLOSED never was archived.
