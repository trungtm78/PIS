## The path of a release

1. A model is registered (PROPOSED) and **approved by someone other than the person who registered it** (`approveAiModel`, `llm.provider.approve`, `403 SELF_APPROVAL_DENIED`). A model that is not APPROVED can be in no profile that is released (`422 MODEL_NOT_APPROVED`).
2. A **prompt version** is drafted (the template stays in the document store: only its key and its SHA-256 are kept, never the text; both schemas are object schemas that name their properties). A draft can be revised; **an approved version cannot** (`409 PROMPT_IMMUTABLE`, and the database refuses the update too). Another person approves it (`approveAiPromptVersion`): it becomes APPROVED with the label `PRODUCTION` and the version that had the label becomes RETIRED. The author cannot approve their own version.
3. An **evaluation dataset** is a numbered version of synthetic or anonymised cases (`case_code`, `input_ref`, `expected`, optional `classification` PUBLIC or INTERNAL and `tags`). A case above internal is real data that was not made harmless and is refused (`409 EVAL_CASE_CLASSIFICATION_INVALID`); up to 500 cases; a version does not change. A dataset is usable for a release only if it has at least one case tagged `injection`.
4. A **profile** is drafted for a task: the model it runs, the models it may use (the fallbacks), effort, caps (output, input, time, concurrency), the read-only tools it may call (`422 TOOL_NOT_READ_ONLY` for anything else), the highest data class, the routes it may use and the thresholds, the prompt it runs, the profile to go back to and the retention. The international route is kept to public data (`422 AI_ROUTE_NOT_ALLOWED`, the baseline of the classification matrix).
5. An **evaluation** is started for one profile, one dataset and one route (`startAiEvalRun`, `llm.eval.manage`). The profile must be a draft or waiting for evaluation (it becomes EVAL_PENDING), the route must be one of the routes of the profile, the dataset must be of the same task and have an injection case, an international run needs a public dataset, and the provider and the model of the route must be active. The runner gives the metrics; they are held against the thresholds (default: citation precision at least 0.95, unsupported claims at most 0.02, injection resistance 1, schema validity at least 0.99; a profile cannot weaken them). A run that the runner could not finish is kept as FAILED and cannot be signed (`422 EVAL_NOT_FINISHED`); a new run of the same profile, dataset and route sets the older one aside (SUPERSEDED).
6. An **expert signs** the run (`signAiEvalRun`, `llm.eval.sign`) with a comment of ten characters at the version it was read at. The person who started the run cannot sign it. It can be signed PASS only if every metric passed (`SIGNED_PASS`), and FAIL otherwise (`SIGNED_FAIL`).
7. **Release** (`post_model_profiles_id_publish`, `llm.publish`; the body is the `Decision` of the contract with a reason and the ids of the evidence runs). The gate is judged in this order: every model of the profile is APPROVED (`422 MODEL_NOT_APPROVED`), the effort is one the models take (`UNSUPPORTED_PARAMETER`), the profile has runs (`EVAL_REQUIRED`), **every route has a current run** (`EVAL_MISSING_FOR_ROUTE`) that is **signed as passed** (`EVAL_NOT_PASSED`), and the person who drafted the profile is not the one who releases it (`SELF_APPROVAL_DENIED`). Every refusal of the gate is `422`, the self-approval `403`. Only then the profile is PUBLISHED, the profile in force for the task is SUPERSEDED and becomes the one to go back to if none was named, and the audit row keeps the ids of the runs that were the evidence. `return` sends a draft or a waiting profile back to DRAFT; `reject` is not a decision of this step (`422`). One profile of a task is PUBLISHED at a time (a partial unique index).

## Screens

- **LLM-10** lists the versions (task, name, revision, the start of the hash, label, state), approves a draft, revises a draft (the template key is asked for again) and drafts a version; the note says that an approved version does not change.
- **LLM-12** lists the datasets with source, highest class and the number of cases and of injection cases, and has a form that takes the cases as JSON and refuses what the server would refuse (not a list, no case, more than 500, a case without its fields, a case above internal).
- **LLM-04** lists the profiles with their model, routes, class, effort and state, links each to its release, and has the form to draft one from the approved models and the approved prompts only (the form does not send while they are loading or could not be read).
- **LLM-17** takes a profile (`?profile=`) and shows for each route the state of its evaluation (not run, waiting for an expert, did not finish, signed as passed, signed as failed, or signed as passed over metrics that did not pass: not usable), the metrics that failed, the cost, and the forms to run an evaluation (datasets of the same task with an injection case; public ones for the international route), to sign a run and to release or send back. The release button is off while a route has no signed pass or a model is not approved, and the server judges it again. Every screen offers nothing to act with to who does not hold the permission and in a read-only session, and has the standard states.

## Assumptions and limits

- **The runner is a mock** (`MockEvalRunner`) until the gateway (ST-LLM-005) calls the models through LiteLLM; the port, the thresholds, the signature and the gate are final. The cost of an evaluation is kept on the run (`cost_vnd`) but is **not yet taken from a budget**: the accounting of evaluation spend comes with the gateway.
- `rollback_profile_id` is the profile to go back to; the act of rolling back (`ROLLED_BACK`) and the routing and fallback of a request (LLM-05) belong to ST-LLM-005 and ST-LLM-006.
- The comparison of evaluations of two models (LLM-13) and the playground (LLM-11) are other stories. The template of a prompt is uploaded through the document store, not here: the screen takes its key and its hash.
- The permissions `llm.prompt.read`, `llm.eval.read`, `model_profile_version.read` and `ai_eval_run.read` are tenant-wide: who holds them on a unit only is refused. The profile form reads the model catalogue (`llm.provider.read`) and the prompts (`llm.prompt.read`), so a role that drafts profiles needs both; without them the form says so and sends nothing.
