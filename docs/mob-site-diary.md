# MOB: contracts, milestones and the site diary on the phone (ST-MOB-020, screens MOB-61 and MOB-62)

A contractor who was invited as a guest, or a supervisor, sees the contracts they work on and their payment milestones (MOB-61) and writes the daily site diary on the phone, with or without coverage (MOB-62), for signing. The behaviour is `MOB.md` (L4, BR-MOB-02, BR-MOB-06) and `QMS.md` 3.1; the operations are the ones of the web (`get_resources_contract`, `get_resources_boq_line`, `get_resources_site_diary`, `saveSiteDiaryDraft`, `reviseSiteDiaryDraft`, `signSiteDiaryByContractor`, `correctSiteDiary`, `resolveDiaryConflict`), so the web (CTR, QMS-01 to QMS-03, QMS-09) and the phone agree on the fields, the limits and the words. The code is `apps/mobile/lib/features/site_diary`; the camera, the position and the photograph pipeline are the shared tools of `lib/features/field_evidence` (`docs/mob-field-issues.md`). It extends the diary of ST-QMS-002 with the scope of a contractor; it does not re-implement the queue, the draft store or the uploader.

## Who sees what

The scope is the server's. A guest contractor is invited to contracts; `get_resources_contract` lists those and nothing else, and a contract outside the scope is `404 RESOURCE_NOT_FOUND`, the same answer as for a contract that does not exist (UAT-AC365). The phone filters nothing and the session carries no list of rights: MOB-61 lists what the server gives, `MOB-61?contract=<id>` and MOB-62 for a contract the reader may not open show the state **error with the code RESOURCE_NOT_FOUND** and nothing of that contract. What a reader may do with a diary is what the server says in `actions` and `signable_roles` for that reader (`revise`, `contractorSign`, `correct`); a screen offers exactly those buttons and the server judges each command again, with the scope of the contract.

## MOB-61: contracts and milestones

The list shows each contract with its status, project, contractor and completion day, and opens the diary. The **milestones** of a contract are the lines of its bill of quantities of the revision in force that name a milestone (`milestone_code`, `milestone_due_on`, CTR 4: in a lump-sum contract a line is a payment milestone), in the order of the due day; they are read when the person opens them, not with the list. The server gives no state for a milestone, and the clock of the phone is never used to judge a deadline, so the screen shows the due day and nothing it cannot cite.

## MOB-62: the diary of a day

A diary belongs to a contract and a day (`?date=` or today in Vietnam time: the phone's clock only proposes the day, it never judges a licence or a deadline; a day that has not come is refused by the form and by the server). The fields are the ones of the web: the work of the day (required, at most 8000 characters), the work items (one per line, at most 50 of at most 200 characters; it is a field of the content like the others, and a field the form cannot show is kept and signed with the rest), the weather, the workforce, the notes, the position (with its consent) and the photographs.

* **A draft per person, contract and day** lives in the encrypted store and is written at every change, so closing the application loses nothing.
* **Save queues one command** `site_diary.append` (`docs/mob-sync.md`) whose idempotency key *is* the identity of the save (`client_operation_id`). A day with no diary yet is a new draft of the contract and the day; a diary that exists is written over under the version that was read (the payload names `diary_id`, the command is based on `server_version`, `If-Match` on the server). Three sends of one save by retry, with the answer lost twice, are one diary: the key is the same on every send, the server carries it out once and the phone looks the command up by its key (UAT-AC358, `diary_screen_test.dart`). A second tap on Save with the same text is the same command; a changed text replaces the command that has not left the phone (the first is discarded, the change gets a new identity); a save that is being sent, whose answer was lost, or that waits for a choice blocks another save until it is settled, because another save could be a second diary. What is written after a save the server took is still the person's unsent work: the draft carries an `edited` mark from the first change after the save was queued, and reopening the screen shows the server's diary only for a draft that was not touched since its accepted save. A draft that cannot be written to the phone is never queued from its older stored copy: Save stops with the "draft cannot be saved" notice and keeps the form (the same holds for MOB-63 and MOB-64).
* **Two editors of one diary** (UAT-AC366): the second revision, based on a version that is no longer the diary, comes back `VERSION_CONFLICT`; the command waits in `CONFLICT`, the diary shows **Choose a version**, and MOB-42 shows both versions field by field (`content.summary`, `content.workforce_count`, ...) through the adapter this feature declares in `MobileFeature.conflictAdapters` (a small seam added to the foundation: the provider of the conflict adapters collects the adapters of the features). Nothing is overwritten; the choice goes back on the version the server holds, with a new key, and is carried out once.
* **The position** is kept only with the consent of the person (BR-MOB-06, UAT-AC367): without it the content has neither `location` nor `geo_consent`; with it and a measured position the content carries `geo_consent: true` and `location {lat, lng, accuracy_m}` (the server refuses a place without the consent). Taking the consent back takes the position, and the place and time of the photographs, with it.
* **The photographs** are prepared on the phone (type checked, large ones compressed, place and time removed without the consent) and handed to the uploader once each; the diary shows what happens to each.

### Signing and correcting: online, on the diary as the server holds it

The contractor's signature (`signSiteDiaryByContractor`, method `IN_APP_OTP`, `If-Match` the version read) and the correction of a signed diary (`correctSiteDiary`, with its reason) are **never queued and never made from a copy**: offline, from a copy, in a read-only system or while a change of this diary has not reached the server, the button is disabled with the reason beside it. The identifier of the signature (or of the correction) is the key of the attempt, so a retry after a lost answer is the same signature and the diary holds one (UAT-AC358). After the contractor signs, the content cannot be edited (`DIARY_STATE_INVALID`, the content hash is over the content): the screen shows the content as it was signed, each signature (a signature that is no longer over the content is shown void), and offers a **correction**, a new revision with its reason that must be signed again; the signed diary keeps its content and signatures (UAT-AC356). The value of an in-app confirmation is not settled by the business (CXN-EXE-05), and the screen says so beside the button.

## What only a device (or the service) can prove

The camera, gallery and position plugins and the real Exif of both platforms (`docs/mob-field-issues.md`); the second factor that `MFA_REQUIRED` asks for before a signature (the screen tells the person to verify and try again; the flow is MOB-03); TalkBack and VoiceOver on the two screens (the tests check labels, tap targets, contrast and 200 percent text on the host); the whole of UAT-AC365, UAT-AC358, UAT-AC366 and UAT-AC356 against the real server with two real phones; the tenant switch with a diary in the queue.

## Gaps and decisions (for the lead)

* **The photographs reach the server as clean attachments of the device, not as photographs of the diary.** `registerSitePhoto` takes the key of an object of the file service and `reviseSiteDiaryDraft` takes no attachment; nothing consumes the id of a clean `sync_attachment`. The content of the diary does not depend on the photographs, and the screen says so.
* **No milestone state**: the contract design has no state of a milestone, only its code and due day.
* The conflict screen MOB-42 names a field by its path (`content.summary`), as it does for any field it has no name for.
* `?date=` and the day shown at the opening come from the clock of the phone, in Vietnam time; this is the one use of the clock, as on the web.
* A queue command found `UNKNOWN` because the network was cut while it was sent (ST-MOB-005) shows "Chưa xác định kết quả" until the next lookup; for a diary written in a tunnel that is the first thing the person sees.
