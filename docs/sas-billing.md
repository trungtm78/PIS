# Billing run and e-invoice (ST-SAS-011, SAS.md 3.7, BR-SAS-04, BR-SAS-13, BR-SAS-14)

Each billing period of a subscription has exactly one original invoice; it is issued to the buyer through the port of the e-invoice provider; an issued invoice is never changed or cancelled, and a subscription that does not pay is only marked past due. Money is whole VND, the VAT is rounded half up line by line, and nothing about the tenant or its license changes because an invoice is late.

## Commands and reads

* `runBillingCycle` (`POST /system/billing-runs`, 202, `system.billing.manage`): `period_start`, `period_end` and optionally `tenant_ids` (all the Active tenants when none). For every live subscription (Active or PastDue) whose billing period, counted from its anchor and cycle, is **exactly** that period, it makes the Draft original invoice, or finds the one the period already has: a second run makes none. A period that is backwards, has not begun (Vietnam date) or is longer than 365 days is 422 `BILLING_PERIOD_INVALID`; a tenant that does not exist is 404. A period that ended inside a trial makes no invoice, and one that the trial ended in is billed from the day the trial ended (the plan for those days, rounded down, and the use from that day); a subscription with nothing to bill (a plan that costs nothing and no use beyond its limits) makes none either. The command acts as each tenant in turn, so every invoice, audit row and event belongs to its tenant.
* `issueEInvoice` (`POST /system/invoices/{invoiceId}/issue`, 202, `If-Match`, a reason of 10 characters or more): a Draft or an `IssueFailed` invoice becomes PendingIssue and the first attempt at the provider is made at once, with the reference key = the invoice id. The answer is `ISSUED` (the invoice is Issued: number, template, series, tax authority code, lookup code), `PENDING_ISSUE` (the provider did not answer: the worker asks again) or `ISSUE_FAILED`. Any other status is 409 `EINVOICE_STATE_INVALID` (a second issue of an invoice that went out), a stale `If-Match` is 409 `VERSION_CONFLICT`.
* `listTenantInvoices` (`GET /system/tenants/{tenantId}/invoices`, `system.tenant.read`, change request CR-SAS-14: the contract had no read of invoices for SAS-09): the latest 24 invoices with their lines, the status of the subscription, `past_due_since` and `suspension_proposed_at`.
* `get_resources_invoice_line` and `get_resources_invoice_line_id` (`invoice_line.read`, the tenant plane): the lines of the caller's own tenant; a line of another tenant is 404.
* There is **no operation that cancels an invoice**, in the contract or in the database (no column, no function, no status): a mistake in an issued invoice is an adjustment or a replacement that points to it (SAS-24, a later story).

## The invoice

* Lines (`invoice_line`): `SUBSCRIPTION` (the base price of the plan for the period, with the VAT category of the plan), `USAGE` (for each METERED feature of the plan with an overage price: the use in the period beyond the limit, counted by the days of Vietnam, times the price, rounded half up) and `ADJUSTMENT` (the proration of a plan change that was applied and not yet charged; the change records the invoice it went onto, so it is charged once). `vat_vnd` is rounded half up per line (a negative line takes the VAT of its own size with the opposite sign); `subtotal` = sum of the amounts, `vat` = sum of the VAT, `total` = subtotal + vat (a table CHECK). The due day is the end of the period plus the `due_days` of the payment terms (15 when there is none).
* The database keeps the rules whatever the application does (migration V1485): one ORIGINAL per (subscription, period) (unique index, a Discarded draft does not count); the lines are written while the invoice is a Draft and never after; an invoice goes out for issue only when its lines add up to its totals; an issued invoice is never edited (totals, lines, buyer, number, dates: `EINVOICE_IMMUTABLE`) or deleted; the status moves only along Draft -> PendingIssue -> Issued -> Sent -> PartiallyPaid -> Paid (Overdue on the way, Adjusted or Replaced at the end), PendingIssue -> IssueFailed -> PendingIssue, Draft -> Discarded (`EINVOICE_STATE_INVALID`); one e-invoice number per provider, template and series.
* The buyer is kept on the invoice as a snapshot (the code and name of the tenant).

## The provider of e-invoices

`EInvoiceProviderPort.issue(request)` takes the invoice with the reference key and returns the number, template, series, tax authority code and lookup code; it must answer the same key with the same invoice and never issue a second one [CXN-INV-03]. The adapter is the **MOCK** (`MockEInvoiceProvider`, one instance per process shared by the commands and the worker) until a contract with a provider exists (D05/D06): its number is worked out from the reference key, so the same key is the same invoice in every process and after every restart, and it keeps one invoice per key. A provider that does not answer (`EInvoiceProviderUnavailable`) is asked again 10 minutes after the first failure, then after 1, 4 and 12 hours: five attempts within 24 hours of the first; then the invoice is `IssueFailed` (event `sas.invoice.issue_failed.v1`, alert `SAS_EINVOICE_ISSUE_FAILED`) and a Billing Operator issues it again.

## The job `einvoice-billing` (every 5 minutes)

Per tenant, through the control role acting as the tenant: (1) makes the Draft invoice of the period that closed last of each live subscription (the daily billing; the second pass finds it); (2) asks the provider again for the invoices whose attempt is due; (3) marks an invoice Overdue the day after its due day (Vietnam date) and its subscription PastDue (`past_due_since`); after 30 days past due it records `suspension_proposed_at` once (audit `billing.suspension_proposed`, event `sas.billing.suspension_proposed.v1`). It changes nothing about the tenant or its license: only a System Admin decides `suspendTenant` with a reason (BR-SAS-04, UAT-AC37). It never issues a Draft by itself: that is the decision of a person.

## Screen SAS-09

`BillingScreen`: the tenant picker (as SAS-07), the form of the run (period start and end, checked as the server does), the invoices with their status, total, due day and e-invoice number and, one click away, their lines; an issue form with a reason; the notices of a subscription past due and of a proposed suspension (the screen has no way to suspend). While an invoice waits for the provider the page reads again every 15 seconds. The strings are `sas.billing.*` in Vietnamese and English.

## Assumptions and what is not yet there

* The invoice is billed for the period **named**; the plan fee is the base price of the plan for the period, and the use is counted over the same period, so a run made before the period ends bills the use so far (the daily job bills only closed periods).
* The overage price of a unit of a METERED feature is the new column `plan_feature.overage_price_vnd` (written while the plan version is a draft). Nothing in the plan catalogue contract writes it yet (see TODOS).
* The VAT category of every line is the one of the plan (default `NOT_SUBJECT`, "KCT") until the rules of issuance are confirmed (CXN-INV-01).
* The buyer snapshot has the code and name of the tenant only; the tax code and address of the buyer are not modelled yet (CXN-INV-02).
