## The operations

| Operation | Who (on the whole tenant) | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `createAiKnowledgeCollection` `POST /ai-knowledge-collections` | `llm.knowledge.manage` | Makes a collection for an embedding model of the tenant and the highest classification it takes. 422 `MODEL_NOT_APPROVED` (the model is not APPROVED), 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED` (the model does not embed - `capabilities.embedding` - or is cleared for less than the collection takes), 404 for a model the tenant does not have, 409 `DUPLICATE_KEY` for a code taken. |
| `indexAiKnowledgeItem` `POST /ai-knowledge-collections/{id}/items` | `llm.knowledge.manage` | Indexes a document version into the collection, or indexes it again (the chunks it had are replaced). 409 `SCAN_PENDING` while the version waits to be scanned, 422 `FILE_NOT_CLEAN` when it is infected, 422 `CLASSIFICATION_ABOVE_COLLECTION`, 409 `STAGE_TRANSITION_DENIED` for a collection that is not ACTIVE, 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED` when the version has no text recorded, the text was changed after the OCR, or it is longer than 2,000 chunks. |
| `listAiKnowledgeCollections`, `getAiKnowledgeCollection` | `llm.knowledge.read` | The collections and one collection with its items: the hash of the access list, the chunks, the classification and `stale` (the document carries another access list than the one the item was indexed with). |

## Indexing

The text of a version is the text the OCR of the document module recorded (`document_page_ocr`, read from the object store, its hash checked). A page is cut into chunks of at most 1,200 characters (paragraphs kept together, a longer one cut at a space, a chunk never across two pages), each chunk is embedded by the model of the collection (the port `AiEmbeddingPort`; a stand-in until the gateway goes through LiteLLM) and stored with its page, its number, the hash of its text, the key of its text in the object store, its classification and the hash of the access list it was read with.

## Who may read, and retrieval

Who may read a document is decided by the document module, not by a list of the knowledge: the scope of the reads of document versions (`versionScope`, permission `document_version.read`) - the unit that issues the document or, for an incoming letter, the unit of its register, the unit it was given to and who took it in. Retrieval (`AiKnowledgeService.retrieve`, which takes the `EffectiveAccess` of the asker) embeds the question with the model of the collection and asks the table for the nearest chunks with the tenant, the collection and that scope **before** the distance. A sealed proposal (`subject_module = BID_PROPOSAL`) is never indexed nor offered. The `acl_hash` of an item and of its chunks is the hash of what governs the reading of the document - its classification, its issuer unit and, for a letter, the register unit, the unit it was given to and who took it in; a chunk is a candidate only while the document still hashes to it, so a document whose governing unit or classification changed is withheld from everybody until it is indexed again (the job `llm-knowledge-reindex` does it within minutes; the screen shows the items that wait). A grant that is revoked takes the documents away at once. Where pgvector is installed the distance is `<=>` over the HNSW index of the embeddings; the TEST profile keeps the vectors as plain arrays and computes the cosine in SQL. The log of a retrieval (`llm.knowledge.retrieved`) holds the hash of the question and the chunks and document versions that were returned, never the question and never an id the asker may not read.
