# MOB: AI on the phone, cited questions, findings one to a card, the source viewer (ST-MOB-030)

An expert or a leader asks about the bids of a package and is answered only with citations in their own scope (MOB-37), reads what the model proposed for each criterion of a contractor, one finding to a card (MOB-38), and opens the cited page at its source (MOB-39). It reads what the web already shows (BID-07 to BID-09, the gateway rule of LLM) and agrees with it: **everything the model says is a proposal for an expert to verify, every citation is verified by the server, and nothing is acted on from the phone.** The code is in `apps/mobile/lib/features/ai_review`; there is no server change.

## The rules

1. **A proposal, always labelled.** Each card and each screen carries "AI proposal - expert review required" (the sentence of the web). The status the model gave (`SUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED`, `MISSING`, `NEEDS_REVIEW`) and what a person decided (`final_status`) are two lines, never merged (BR-BID-09). The signal (`confidence_type`) is worded as a kind of signal, never as a probability (BR-BID-08).
2. **Needs verification.** A finding that no person has decided carries "Needs verification" (AC-ST-MOB-030-2); a finding that claimed without a citation found on its page carries "Missing source" (BR-BID-07, the server's flag). The phone never judges either.
3. **Nothing to approve.** A card offers to open a citation and nothing else: no approve, reject, accept or apply button, no `Dismissible`, no swipe, no action in a notification. A test drags a card both ways and finds no request and no change; it also finds none of the button widgets and none of the words. The review is the expert's act on the web, and the card says so.
4. **Every citation shows the server's verdict.** `verified_present` is "Found on the page (checked by the server)" or "Not found on the page". The mark on the page text is only a convenience for reading (white space ignored, `locateQuote`); words that are not in the text are not marked at all.
5. **Refusal for lack of citation (AC-ST-MOB-030-1, UAT-AC466).** A question is answered with the passages nearest to it in the files the person may read, each with its file and page. When retrieval returns nothing in the person's scope there is no answer: the card says the answer is refused for want of a citation in scope, with the code `REJECTED_NO_CITATION` of the gateway rule. Nothing is guessed and no text is generated by the phone.
6. **Access judged again at the source (AC-ST-MOB-030-3, UAT-PLAN-43).** MOB-39 reads the finding (or the proposals of the package) first and the page second, under the permission of that moment. A revoked person, a citation that is not on their finding, a sealed or unreleased proposal and a missing record all give the same 404 and the same sentence ("Content not found") and nothing of the page is read; a person who may read the finding but not the page text (403) sees the quotation only.
7. **Nothing kept.** The text of a bid is confidential. The AI screens do not use the read cache (`FreshRead` instead of `DataView`): every opening asks the server, with no connection the screen says it is offline instead of showing what was read last time, and nothing is written to the device. A question is typed into a field, sent as the query of one request, and is in no location, no copy and no log; the thread lives in memory for as long as the screen.

## The screens

| screen | location | reads |
|---|---|---|
| MOB-37 | `/projects/mob-37?package=<id>` (without it: the packages of the scope, `get_resources_procurement_package`) | `listAiModels` (the newest approved model that embeds), `searchProposalPassages` (5 passages) |
| MOB-38 | `/projects/mob-38?run=<id>` (without it: the runs, `get_resources_analysis_run`) | `listAnalysisFindings` (up to 4 pages of 50, a note when there are more) |
| MOB-39 | `?run=&finding=&evidence=` for a citation of a finding, `?package=&doc=&page=` for a passage of a question | the finding as above, or `get_resources_proposal` (`access` FULL, not locked), then `listProposalOcrPages` for the file |

The states are the eight of `StateBoundary`, plus the not-found card (404 is never drawn as an empty or a generic error: it must not tell a revoked person anything). Asking is disabled offline, with the reason beside the button; nothing is queued, and a question is a read, so a retry is simply asking again.

## Gaps (honest)

- **The embedding model.** `searchProposalPassages` requires `embedding_model_id`, and the phone finds it through `listAiModels`, which needs `llm.provider.read` (the whole tenant). A leader or an expert without that right (the register answers 403) gets the sentence "no approved search model your account can use" (`EMBEDDING_MODEL_UNAVAILABLE`) and nothing is asked: that is not shown as a refusal of the question, and any other failure of the register (a network or a server error) stays a failure to retry. No operation hands the approved retrieval model to a person who holds only `bid.evaluate`, so the server change needed is either to make `embedding_model_id` of `searchProposalPassages` optional (the server then uses the tenant's approved retrieval model) or an operation readable under `bid.evaluate` that returns that model's id. A server default for the approved retrieval model (optional `embedding_model_id`) is the fix; it is a contract change and is not made here. `EmbeddingModelResolver` is the seam a tenant can override meanwhile.
- **No generated answer.** The gateway keeps neither the text of the question nor of the answer and has no read of an answer, so MOB-37 answers with the retrieved passages themselves (extractive), not with a summary written by the model. A generated, cited answer needs a server operation that returns it with its citations; the refusal rule above would apply to it unchanged.
- **Retrieval always returns the nearest passages** when the person can read any page of the package; relevance is not judged by the phone (no threshold is invented). "Nothing in scope" is the only refusal the phone can know.
- **Criterion names.** A card shows the criterion id; the names of the criteria need a read of the criteria version, which the screen does not make yet.
- **The comparison of proposals of several contractors** moves to the web or a tablet (MOB.md L6).

## Tests

`test/features/ai_review/ai_review_test.dart` names `AC-ST-MOB-030-1` to `-4`, `UAT-AC466`, `UAT-PLAN-42`, `UAT-PLAN-43`, `UI-MOB-37`, `UI-MOB-38` and `UI-MOB-39`: the refusal, the passages with the query that was sent, no copy kept, offline, a retry, a denied and a hidden package, the missing model; the cards, the two lines for the proposal and the decision, no control and no gesture, the filters; the order of the two reads, the 404 of a revoked person without a read of the page, the 403 on the page text, the unverified citation, a page that waits for a person; the seven states and the screen contract (tap targets on both platforms, labels, contrast, three sizes at 200 percent text) of each screen; both languages. Mutation checks (break, see red, restore, `cmp`) were made on: the refusal on an empty retrieval, "needs verification", the 404 handling, the marking of the quotation, the server's verdict, the offline gate of asking, the choice of the newest model, and the refusal of a citation that is not on a page.

## What only a device can prove

Reading the findings and the page text on a real phone with TalkBack and VoiceOver (the order in which a card is read, the citation rows as buttons), the look of the marked quotation at 200 percent text on a 360 dp screen, a long page of OCR text and its scrolling cost, the behaviour of a request cut between the finding and the page, and the screenshot blocking and background snapshot of the OS for a screen that shows a confidential bid (the application sets none yet; that is a release decision for Security).
