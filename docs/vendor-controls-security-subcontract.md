# Bid security and subcontracts (lane VND-2, slice beta: research V10 and V11)

Rules in `packages/kernel/src/vendor-security.ts` and `vendor-subcontract.ts` (pure, golden tests in `packages/kernel/test/vendor-controls.test.ts`), tables in `db/migrations/V2424` to `V2427`, stores `packages/db/src/vendor-security-store.ts` and `vendor-subcontract-store.ts`, alert source `home-vendor-beta-sources.ts`, controllers `apps/api/src/modules/bid/vendor-security.controller.ts` and `vendor-subcontract.controller.ts`, screens VND-09 (`apps/web/src/screens/vnd/security/`) and VND-10 (`apps/web/src/screens/vnd/subcontracts/`). Every number below is read from a rule of governance (`VENDOR_BID_SECURITY`, `VENDOR_SUBCONTRACT`, `VENDOR_REPUTATION`) through `VendorPolicyReader`, flagged **UNVERIFIED** with its formula, source and as-of day until the legal staff of the tenant confirm it against the Gazette (Điều 16, 20 Luật Đấu thầu 22/2023 and NĐ 214/2025 as read from summaries).

## 1. Bid security (V10, VND-09)

A bid security (`vendor_bid_security`: a bank guarantee, an insurance bond or a cash deposit) is **received** by one person, **confirmed with the issuer** by another (with the reference the issuer gave; CHECK in the database too), and ends **released**, **expired**, **forfeited** or **rejected**. States: RECEIVED, CONFIRMED, REJECTED, FORFEIT_PROPOSED, FORFEITED, RELEASED, EXPIRED. The machine is the kernel's (`moveSecurity`); every move is a row of the append-only ledger `vendor_bid_security_event`; the identity of a security (package, bidder, issuer, number, amount, days, required amount, factor) never changes (guard trigger) and a final state stays final.

**What a package asks.** `vendor_package_control` (slice alpha) holds the base amount and the last day the bids are valid. The amount a bidder must lodge is the base times the factor of the ledger of bid conduct of VND-1 (`bidSecurityFactor`: three times for two years after a posted event; rule `VENDOR_REPUTATION`), held until the last day of the bid plus `validity_margin_days` (default 30). The required amount and the factor are kept on the row as of the day it was received. `GET /vendor/bid-securities?package_id=&contractor_id=` also answers a `standing`: the amount, the factor, the day it must hold until and the gaps (MISSING, NOT_CONFIRMED, AMOUNT_BELOW, VALIDITY_SHORT, EXPIRED, FORFEITED, REJECTED). A security nobody confirmed satisfies nothing when `require_confirmation` is set; the shortfall of the amount blocks (`amount_below_enforcement` BLOCK, or only warns on WARN).

**Forfeiture.** Proposed by one person for a reason of the law (`WITHDREW_AFTER_CLOSING`, `WITHDREW_BEFORE_VALIDITY_END`, `REFUSED_TO_SIGN`, `NO_PERFORMANCE_SECURITY`, `FALSE_DECLARATION`, `OTHER_BY_BIDDING_DOCUMENT`) with a reason of 20 characters; withdrawn by the proposer, or decided by **another person** (FORFEIT needs a decision paper and a reason, REFUSE returns the security to CONFIRMED). A forfeiture maps to a behaviour of the ledger of bid conduct (`FORFEIT_BEHAVIOUR`: withdrawing after closing, winning without signing, no performance security); the event `vnd.bid_security.changed.v1` carries `conduct_behaviour` as a **hook**: the investor representative decides whether to record the event of bid conduct, nothing is posted automatically.

**Gate.** `VendorSecurityGate.assertAllowed(tx, { packageId, contractorId, today })` throws 422 `BID_SECURITY_NOT_VALID` (extensions: gaps, required amount, factor, shortfall) only when the package sets a base amount and the assessment enforcement is BLOCK. A package with no requirement is not blocked. The lead wires it into the result of a package (`BidResultStore`); it is not wired by this slice.

**Expiry.** No job writes EXPIRED: the list says when a last day passed (`expiry_facet` FIRST, SECOND, EXPIRED by `first_warning_days` 15 and `second_warning_days` 7) and a person records the expiry or the release. The same facets raise the alert `VENDOR_SECURITY_EXPIRY` (V-A7, audience `vnd.security.confirm`).

## 2. Subcontracts (V11, VND-10)

`vendor_bid_declaration` records what a bid declared about subcontracting: the share of the contract value and the subcontractors it named; a new declaration supersedes the old (never edited, one live row per package and bidder). `vendor_subcontract` is proposed (`declared_in_bid` is read at the proposal), **approved by a person other than the proposer** (CHECK in the database too), and ends REJECTED (a proposer who rejects withdraws) or TERMINATED. At the approval the kernel assessment (`assessSubcontract`) runs again on the day: findings OVER_DECLARED_SHARE, NOT_NAMED_IN_BID, PRIME_SHARE_BELOW_MINIMUM, VALUE_SHARE_MISMATCH, SAME_AS_PRIME, DUPLICATE_SUBCONTRACTOR. The last four block (422 `SUBCONTRACT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`); the first two need an **exception with a reason of 20 characters** from the approver, or block when the rule says BLOCK (`over_declared`, `undeclared_subcontractor`). The prime keeps at least `prime_min_share_pct` (default 51). The gate SUBCONTRACT of VND-1 (restriction in force on the subcontractor or on a party of the contract, unverified tax code, certificate the package asks) runs at the approval. The assessment and the rule version are stored on the row.

**What the prime paid.** `vendor_subcontract_payment`: after a payment request is paid (`payment_request.treasury_paid_on`), the contract manager records the amount, the day and the reference for each approved subcontractor. The record is due `confirm_payment_within_days` (14) after the Treasury paid (DUE_SOON in the last 3 days, OVERDUE after); `confirmed_by_subcontractor` is the hook for the supplier portal of VND-3. A sum paid above the value of the subcontract is shown as `over_paid` and raises the alert. Alerts: `VENDOR_SUBCONTRACT_BREACH` (V-A9: an approved exception, an over-paid subcontract, a subcontractor under a restriction; audience `vnd.subcontract.approve`) and `VENDOR_SUB_PAYMENT_CONFIRM` (audience `vnd.subcontract.pay`).

## 3. Operations and permissions

Reads (`vnd.read`): `listVendorBidSecurities`, `getVendorBidSecurity`, `listVendorSubcontracts`. Commands (all idempotent, audited CONFIDENTIAL, outbox event with ids and codes only): `receiveVendorBidSecurity` and `releaseVendorBidSecurity` (`vnd.security.write`), `confirmVendorBidSecurity` and `rejectVendorBidSecurity` (`vnd.security.confirm`), `proposeVendorBidSecurityForfeiture` and `withdrawVendorBidSecurityForfeiture` (`vnd.security.forfeit.propose`), `decideVendorBidSecurityForfeiture` (`vnd.security.forfeit.decide`), `declareVendorBidSubcontracting`, `proposeVendorSubcontract` and `rejectVendorSubcontract` (`vnd.subcontract.write`), `approveVendorSubcontract` and `terminateVendorSubcontract` (`vnd.subcontract.approve`), `recordVendorSubcontractPayment` (`vnd.subcontract.pay`). All grants are on the whole tenant (403 `SCOPE_DENIED` otherwise). Error codes: `BID_SECURITY_NOT_VALID` (422), `SUBCONTRACT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (422), `SUBCONTRACT_STATE_INVALID` (409); the moves reuse `STAGE_TRANSITION_DENIED`, `SELF_APPROVAL_DENIED`, `REASON_REQUIRED`, `VERSION_CONFLICT`.

## 4. Indicators and hooks

Figures `VENDOR_SECURITY_CONFIRMED` (confirmed over received and not rejected) and `VENDOR_SUB_PAYMENT_CONFIRMED` (recorded in time over recorded plus overdue) are computed by `securityFigures` and `subcontractFigures`. For the risk grade of slice gamma: `securityForfeitCount(tx, contractorId)` and `subcontractBreachCount(tx, contractorId, today)`. Not done here: the call of `VendorSecurityGate` at the result of a package, the supplier portal confirmation of a payment (VND-3), the posting of a forfeiture as an event of bid conduct.
