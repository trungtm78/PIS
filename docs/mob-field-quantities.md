# MOB: quantities with evidence and the field quality inspection (ST-MOB-021, screens MOB-63 and MOB-64)

A supervisor or a field engineer records the quantity measured on site with photographs (MOB-63) and answers the checklist of a quality inspection (MOB-64), on the phone, with or without coverage, so that the acceptance rests on dated, located evidence. The behaviour is `MOB.md` (L4, BR-MOB-02, BR-MOB-06), `CTR.md` (BR-CTR measurements) and `QMS.md` 3.2; the operations are the ones of the web (`recordMeasurement`, `submitMeasurement`, `get_resources_boq_line`, `get_resources_measurement`, `listInspections`, `recordInspectionResult`, `createQualityAcceptance`, `get_resources_quality_nonconformance`), so the web (CTR-10, QMS-04, QMS-05) and the phone agree on the fields, the limits and the words. The code is `apps/mobile/lib/features/field_quantities` (both screens); the photograph pipeline, the position with its consent and the photograph flags are `lib/features/field_evidence` (`docs/mob-field-issues.md`).

## MOB-63: a quantity with its evidence

The form is the record dialog of CTR-10: an **item** of the bill of quantities of the revision in force (only the lines the server shows with `boq_item_id`, which it shows to a reader who may record), the **period** (a list; when the reader may not read the periods, the identifier is typed, as on the web), the **quantity** (exact decimal text, above zero, at most six decimals, a point: it is never a float and the digits shown follow the language), the **reference number** of the record, and, only when the reference is already used in the contract, the reason to use it again. The screen shows what is left of the quantity of the item as the server counted it.

* **A draft per contract** in the encrypted store, written at every change.
* **Save queues one command** `measurement.record` whose idempotency key is the identity of the draft (`docs/mob-sync.md`: `POST /contracts/{contract_id}/measurements`). `recordMeasurement` has no client operation id: the operation is idempotent by its key, so a quantity sent twice with the same key, by retry after a lost answer, is **one measurement** (AC-ST-MOB-021-2, UAT-AC358: three sends, one effect, one key; `quantity_screen_test.dart`). Once the record is the queue's the form starts empty for the next quantity; a refusal that the server gave while it waited (`QUANTITY_EXCEEDS_BOQ`, `MEASUREMENT_REFERENCE_DUPLICATE`, `PERIOD_LOCKED`, ...) is told in words beside its code.
* **Sending for approval** (`submitMeasurement`) is made online, on the version that was read, and never queued or made from a copy; a retry after a lost answer keeps the key and submits once. Another person decides (CTR-10).
* **The photographs** follow the consent to keep the position: without it a photograph leaves the phone **without its place and time** (the file is stripped), and the screen shows, before the photograph is sent, the flags it will be registered with, the way QMS-10 asks: **`gps_source` NONE, `geo_consent` false**, and the missing place and time as **warnings only** — a photograph is never refused for what it does not say (AC-ST-MOB-021-1, UAT-AC367). With the consent and a position of the phone the source is `DEVICE_AT_CAPTURE`; with the consent and a place inside the file, `EXIF`. The type is checked before anything is kept (a script renamed to `.jpg` is refused with its reason, no upload request is made).

## MOB-64: the result of a field inspection

The checklist the inspection was given is answered item by item (pass, fail, not applicable), the overall result (pass, fail, pass with conditions) and its words are written, with photographs and the position under the same consent. The rules are the web's (QMS-04): every item is answered once; a pass has no failed item; a fail or a pass with conditions has at least one; a pass with conditions says its condition in the note of each failed item. The result is kept as a draft per inspection and **queued as `inspection.result`** based on the version of the inspection that was read (`POST /inspections/{inspection_id}/result`, `If-Match`; the server half is below). While the result is on its way the form is closed: another one would be a second result. A result based on an older version comes back `VERSION_CONFLICT` and is settled in MOB-42 item by item (the items of the checklist are compared by their code) through the adapter this feature declares; a result refused for a rule (`INSPECTION_STATE_INVALID`, `CHECKLIST_INCOMPLETE`) is told in words beside its code.

**A failed item raises a non-conformance** on the server (QMS 3.2: `recordInspectionResult` creates the NCR; the phone raises nothing). Once the result is recorded the screen shows the non-conformances of the inspection with their severity and whether each blocks the acceptance. **An acceptance request is made online** (`createQualityAcceptance` from an inspection that passed or passed with conditions, with the number of the minutes and the identifier of the file version of the minutes, the scope following the kind of inspection) and **is blocked with 422 `OPEN_CRITICAL_NCR` while a critical non-conformance is open**: the screen says it cannot be accepted yet and why, beside the code (AC-ST-MOB-021-3, UAT-AC361). A closed non-conformance no longer blocks.

## The server half (TK-MOB-021-3)

`inspection.result` is a new type of the sync batch: `packages/kernel/src/sync-command.ts` (`planOf` sends it to `POST /inspections/{id}/result`, versioned; only a UUID goes into the path), the `command_type` enum of `SyncBatchRequest` in `openapi.json` (the contract was regenerated; compat and mobile-pack checks pass), `docs/mob-sync.md`, the kernel test `sync-command.test.ts` and the API test `apps/api/test/sync/sync-batches.test.ts` (accepted over the version read, an older version is a `VERSION_CONFLICT` conflict that is not carried out, a repeated send is answered from its receipt). `measurement.record` existed already. Registering a site photograph and raising a non-conformance are not queued: see the gaps.

## What only a device (or the service) can prove

The camera, gallery and position plugins and the real Exif of both platforms; TalkBack and VoiceOver on the two screens; UAT-AC367, UAT-AC358 and UAT-AC361 against the real server and the real scan; offline quantity and inspection runs with photographs on a real phone (TK-MOB-021-4).

## Gaps and decisions (for the lead)

* **The photographs are not the evidence of the quantity yet.** `recordMeasurement` takes `evidence_refs`, document versions scanned clean of the tenant, and `registerSitePhoto` takes the key of an object of the file service; nothing accepts the id of a clean `sync_attachment`. So a quantity is recorded as a draft with an empty `evidence_refs`, the photographs go up as clean attachments of the device, and submitting is refused with `EVIDENCE_REQUIRED` (told in words) until a file is linked. The flags `gps_source` / `geo_consent` are computed and shown on the phone; the photograph cannot be registered (`registerSitePhoto`) for the same reason. Closing the gap is a server change: accept a clean sync attachment as the object of `registerSitePhoto` and as an evidence reference.
* `raiseNonconformance` and `registerSitePhoto` are not queue commands: a non-conformance is raised by the server from a failed item, and a photograph needs the gap above.
* The acceptance request needs `document_version_id` (the file of the minutes), which the phone cannot upload as a document; it is typed as an identifier, as on the web (QMS-05).
* The directory is `field_quantities` for both screens of the story (`MOB-63` and `MOB-64`).
