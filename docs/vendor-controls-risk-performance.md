# Risk grade and performance of a contractor (VND-2, slice gamma: V13 and V4)

Part of the controls lane VND-2 (`docs/vendor-controls.md`). Code: kernel `packages/kernel/src/vendor-risk.ts`, `vendor-performance.ts`, texts `messages/vnd2c-texts.ts`; db `packages/db/src/vendor-risk-store.ts`, `vendor-risk-signals.ts`, `vendor-performance-store.ts`, `home-vendor-gamma-sources.ts`; api `apps/api/src/modules/bid/vendor-risk.controller.ts`, `vendor-performance.controller.ts`, `vendor-risk-view.ts`; web `apps/web/src/screens/vnd/risk/` (VND-11) and `performance/` (VND-12); migrations `V2428`, `V2429`. Every number below is a rule of governance flagged **UNVERIFIED** (formula, source, as-of day and rule version on every figure through the "i").

## 1. The risk grade (V13, VND-11)

**A grade is a snapshot with its explanation.** `gradeRisk` adds the points of eleven factors, each at most its weight (rule `VENDOR_RISK`): profile gaps (weight times the share missing), restriction (in force: the whole weight; past: a third), posted events of bid conduct, lapsed certificates, alerts on the payee account (shared account, stalled change), consecutive poor ratings, finance below the line, load over capacity, strong independence findings undecided, breaches of the subcontract limits, forfeited securities (a third of the weight each time up to three, except where stated). Tiers LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, CRITICAL begin at `medium_from`, `high_from`, `critical_from`; a restriction in force keeps the tier at or above `restriction_floor`; from `dual_check_from` the grade needs a second person; the next review is `review_months_<tier>` months from the day of the decision. A reading that is unknown adds nothing but profile coverage (unknown counts the whole weight) and finance (insufficient data counts half): **unknown is never a pass**.

**Flow.** `assessVendorRisk` stores a PROPOSED row (`vendor_risk_assessment`, V2428) with the points, readings, inputs, rule version and legal status. At most one row waits and one is confirmed per contractor (partial unique indexes; a second assessment is 409 `RISK_ASSESSMENT_STATE_INVALID`). `decideVendorRiskGrade` (If-Match) confirms or rejects: a grade that needs a dual check is confirmed by a person other than the assessor (403 `SELF_APPROVAL_DENIED`; a CHECK holds it too); a rejection needs a reason of 20 characters (422 `REASON_REQUIRED`). A confirmation writes `contractor.risk_tier`, `risk_next_review_on`, `risk_assessment_id` and supersedes the grade that stood, in one transaction. A snapshot never changes (trigger) and is never deleted. The grade does not decide whether a contractor may bid: it decides how deep the checking goes (`riskTierOf` is the read for the bid evaluation).

**Inputs and the port.** The store reads what the VND-1 slice owns (profile coverage, restrictions, posted events inside the years of the factor, lapsed certificates, payee alerts) and the poor streak of CA-3. The readings that the other slices own come through `VendorRiskSignalPort` (finance status and load of ALPHA, open strong findings of ALPHA, subcontract breaches and security forfeits of BETA). `vendorRiskSignals` in `vendor-risk-signals.ts` is the glue: it ships empty (every reading unknown or zero) and the lead connects the real readers there in one place.

**Screen VND-11.** Register with tier chips (text and icon, not colour alone), score, review day, overdue flag and waiting grade, filters and a cursor pager, two figures with the "i"; a contractor panel with the live grade (points, a bar, the reading, the main reason, the floor), the waiting grade with its decide button (offered only when `can_decide`), the assess button, and the history with the rule version and the unverified flag. States: loading, empty (none and filtered), error with retry, stale, read-only workspace.

## 2. Performance and its publication (V4, VND-12)

The rating itself is the proposal of CA-3 (`contract_rating_proposal`: proposed from the data, told to the contractor by a second person, answered inside the window of `EGP_POSTING_DEADLINES/CONTRACTOR_RESPONSE`, adopted by a third) and the duty of the e-GP lane (`CONTRACTOR_PERFORMANCE`). This slice adds, on top, with the rule `VENDOR_PERFORMANCE`:

- **Publication clock.** An adopted rating is published no later than `publish_within_months` (6) after the contract ended. **The end day** is `terminated_on`, else `completed_on`, for a completion or termination rating (falling back to the `as_of_date` of the rating); `completion_due` for a periodic rating. The day of the limit is still in time. States ON_TIME, DUE_SOON (within `remind_before_days`), OVERDUE, MET, MET_LATE.
- **Record of the posting** (`vendor_performance_publication`, V2429, append-only): `postVendorPerformance` writes the reference the national system gave, by a person other than the one who adopted the rating (403 `SELF_APPROVAL_DENIED`; the trigger reads the adoption too), once for the first posting (409 `PERFORMANCE_PUBLICATION_DUPLICATE`); an update after the warranty or after a correct answer is a new row that supersedes the one before (one successor per row). A rating not adopted is 409 `RATING_NOT_ADOPTED`. The e-GP obligation of the contract is shown **read only** (its state and id): marking the obligation receipted needs its own prepare, submit and receipt flow in the e-GP lane, so the receipt here is not copied into it (a follow-up hook for EGP-2: read `vendor_performance_publication` when it receipts `CONTRACTOR_PERFORMANCE`).
- **Review of the answer.** After the contractor answers, the investor has `review_response_days` (20, our choice) to examine it and update the rating when it is right: NOT_ANSWERED, WITHIN_REVIEW, DUE_SOON, OVERDUE, CLOSED once adopted.
- **Streak.** Consecutive POOR ratings from the latest: a plan to remedy from `poor_streak_remedy` (2), a case for the register of debarment suggested from `poor_streak_restriction` (3). It is a suggestion only: nothing is opened (the screen links to VND-03). The retrospect window is `retrospect_years` (3), `retrospect_years_construction` (6) for contracts of the category CONSTRUCTION.

**Screen VND-12.** Worklist by state (all, due, overdue, posted) with two figures and the rule; per contractor the ratings with the clocks, the streak banner and the retrospect count; the dialog to record a posting (first posting or an update). Same states as VND-11.

## 3. Alerts and indicators

Alert source `VENDOR` (no job; computed when the alert centre is read, `home-vendor-gamma-sources.ts`): `VENDOR_PERFORMANCE_PUBLISH` (DUE_SOON, OVERDUE; audience `vnd.performance.publish`), `VENDOR_PERFORMANCE_RESPONSE` (DUE_SOON, OVERDUE), `VENDOR_POOR_STREAK` (STREAK; audience `vnd.risk.decide`), `VENDOR_RISK_REVIEW` (PENDING: a grade waits for its second person; OVERDUE: a grade in force is past its review day). The owner, expected action and consequence of each are in the alert types of `registerVendorControlsRules`. Indicators (`riskFigures`, `performanceFigures` in the stores): `VENDOR_RISK_REVIEW_IN_TIME`, `VENDOR_HIGH_RISK_CONTRACTORS`, `VENDOR_PERFORMANCE_PUBLISHED_IN_TIME`, `VENDOR_RESPONSE_REVIEWED_IN_TIME` with numerator, denominator and as-of day.

## 4. Permissions, operations, errors

`vnd.read` for the four reads (`getVendorRiskGrade`, `listVendorRiskGrades`, `getVendorPerformance`, `listVendorPerformancePublications`); `vnd.risk.assess` (`assessVendorRisk`), `vnd.risk.decide` (`decideVendorRiskGrade`), `vnd.performance.publish` (`postVendorPerformance`); all on a grant over the whole tenant (a grant on a subtree is 403 `SCOPE_DENIED`). Every command is audited (classification CONFIDENTIAL for the grade, INTERNAL for the publication), idempotent by key, and announces `vnd.risk.assessed.v1`, `vnd.risk.decided.v1`, `vnd.performance.published.v1` with ids and codes only. Error codes: `RISK_ASSESSMENT_STATE_INVALID`, `PERFORMANCE_PUBLICATION_DUPLICATE`, `RATING_NOT_ADOPTED`.
