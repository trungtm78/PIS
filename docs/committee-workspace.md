# Committee workspace (BAN-1)

Status: kernel rules, database, API, report-engine form (this document, migration block BAN-1 2520-2539, spec screens BAN-01 to BAN-07 in `screen_catalog.json`). Web screens, PWA card and phone card follow (see `docs/plans/ban1.md`).

Owner request of 2026-11-08: the head of the provincial steering committee for public investment ("Ban 57", a police officer of the provincial police, unit PA05) runs the province-wide progress of public investment from one workspace. Owner decision of the same day: the chapter is built on the REAL core first and carries NO "simulation" label anywhere (screen, file, pack, phone card, PWA card, figure). Figures come from the core tables through the port `ProvinceFactsReader`; the feed of the Finance Department app (lane FEED-1) writes into the same core, and the provenance of its records is an internal audit column of the core, never part of the contract of this port. The formula, source and as-of behind the "i" of every figure stay: that is governance, not a stamp.

## Decisions

1. **Roles and scope.** Three system roles, granted by IAM over the WHOLE tenant (province-wide READ across every investor unit and sector; row-level security still bounds every query to the tenant, so never across tenants): `COMMITTEE_HEAD` (rank 62), `COMMITTEE_MEMBER` (55), `COMMITTEE_SECRETARIAT` (52). Rights: read of the workspace (`committee.read`), decisions on the attention list (`committee.attention.decide`: head, secretariat), alert acknowledgement (head, secretariat) and evaluation (secretariat), roster management (`committee.manage`: secretariat), directives and meeting minutes of DIR (`directive.*`: head and secretariat manage, head closes and decides extensions; the maker is not the checker constraints of DIR hold), the report engine as the three people of an issue (secretariat prepares, a member checks, the head approves and issues, as the engine has the approver issue). No role holds a capital, payment, disbursement, contract or any other financial write permission (asserted in `packages/db/test/system-roles.test.ts`). Existing tenants get the roles from the privileged migration V2523. Every operation of the workspace checks the permission over the whole tenant (`SCOPE_DENIED` otherwise).
2. **Roster.** `committee` (code, name, legal basis) and `committee_member` (membership, role HEAD/DEPUTY/MEMBER/SECRETARIAT, agency such as PA05, appointing decision, days, `mfa_required`). One active HEAD per committee, one active place per person, nobody appoints themselves, an ended place says why and is never removed. The roster records the appointments; what a person may do is the IAM grant, and the roster says per member whether the matching role is granted (`role_granted`).
3. **Classification and audit of views.** The workspace is `CONFIDENTIAL`; the roster and the audit are `PERSONAL`. EVERY view of a sensitive screen (workspace, attention list, meeting pack, directive tracker, alerts, roster, view audit) writes a row of `committee_view_audit` in the SAME transaction as the read (who, committee role, screen, filters, as-of, feed basis and reference, record count, assurance level); a view that cannot be recorded is not answered; the table is append-only (trigger and revoked privileges) and holds no figure. Reading the audit is audited too. Responses carry `Cache-Control: no-store`. MFA hook: `assurance_level` (UNKNOWN, PASSWORD, MFA) and `mfa_required` are in the row and the DI token `COMMITTEE_ASSURANCE` supplies the level once SSO/MFA exists in the infrastructure phase; today it is UNKNOWN and the audit says so.
4. **The port `ProvinceFactsReader`** (`packages/db/src/province-facts.ts`, shapes in `packages/kernel/src/committee-facts.ts`): `read(tx, { asOf })` returns `ProvinceFacts` (projects, funding lines, units and their reports, a feed note with basis and reference) as of the latest frozen DIS snapshot on or before the day. `CoreProvinceFactsReader` reads: plan, disbursed and the forecast at completion from the snapshot lines (current-year bucket) by project and funding source; the same day and the whole of the year before from the frozen snapshots of the year before (no snapshot there is unknown, a project with no line there is zero); delay days from the baseline in force; stalled days from the idle open steps of the procurement workflow (PRC-1); last report from the current submitted report revisions; reports owed per unit from the open reporting period. Contract for a feed (FEED-1): write into the core tables; if a different reader is wanted, implement `ProvinceFactsReader` (the shapes check themselves with `factsDefects`). Unknown is null, never zero. `MemoryProvinceFactsReader` serves tests that hand facts to the rules; the API tests seed rows of the core.
5. **Figures and the "i".** Ten indicators are definitions of the KPI register (`COMMITTEE_PLAN_TOTAL`, `_DISBURSED`, `_DISBURSEMENT_RATE`, `_DISBURSED_LAST_YEAR`, `_GAP_VS_CURVE`, `_OUTLOOK_LOW`, `_OUTLOOK_HIGH`, `_ATTENTION_PROJECTS`, `_UNITS_NOT_REPORTING`, `_DIRECTIVES_OVERDUE`) with formula, numerator, denominator, source, as-of, owner and the unverified flag; the answers carry `figures` made by the same `figureOf` as the command center (display value, kpi info for the "i", certification, flags). Rows by sector, district, investor and funding source carry plan, disbursed, rate, last year (given only when every line has it, else the coverage is named), change, expected rate of the curve, gap in points, colour, the year-end band and the count of unknown lines.
6. **Rules (all pure, `packages/kernel/src/committee.ts`, thresholds in the governance rule `COMMITTEE_POLICY`, UNVERIFIED, source and as-of in the registry):** expected cumulative curve of the year (interpolated inside the month); colour from the gap to the curve; year-end band = lowest to highest of run-rate, curve-scaled and last-year-ratio estimates, each held at the plan (thin with one method); attention reasons DELAYED, STALLED_PROCEDURE, OVER_BUDGET_RISK, DISBURSEMENT_FAR_BELOW_PLAN, UNIT_NOT_REPORTING with severity 0.5 at the threshold to 1 at twice; impact score = 100 x (sum of weight x severity / sum of weights) x (0.5 + 0.5 x min(1, exposure / reference)); "since last week" lines through `weeklyChanges` of HOME.
7. **Alerts.** `ALERT_TYPE` rules with owner, expected action, consequence and response hours: `COMMITTEE_PROVINCE_BEHIND`, `COMMITTEE_SECTOR_BEHIND`, `COMMITTEE_UNIT_NOT_REPORTING`, `COMMITTEE_DIRECTIVE_OVERDUE`. `evaluateCommitteeAlerts` (secretariat) makes `committee_alert` agree with the rules (one open row per key, refreshed while the condition holds, resolved when it stops; events `committee.alert_raised.v1` and `committee.alert_resolved.v1`); the head acknowledges with a note. The alert centre of HOME reads these rows (lane HUBADAPT, step 4): source `COMMITTEE`, one kind for each alert type, the open and acknowledged rows only, with the severity stored in the row, for the readers who hold `committee.read` on the whole tenant (anybody else only sees what was handed over); the item opens BAN-05. Reader: `packages/db/src/home-risk-committee-sources.ts`.
8. **Directives.** The tracker reads the directives of DIR with the clock of `DIRECTIVE_POLICY` (overdue, escalated per unit). A directive is issued from a project row through `createSteeringMeeting` / `addDirective` of DIR, then `recordAttentionDecision` (DIRECTED with the directive id) links it.
9. **Meeting pack.** Form `BAN-MEETING-PACK` of the report engine (READY, public edition, AS_OF period): headline figures bound to the register and sections for province, sector, district, investor, funding source, attention and directives per unit, Excel with the Word companion produced by the engine, three different people (preparer, checker, approver), the same `CommitteeWorkspaceService` read as the screens.
