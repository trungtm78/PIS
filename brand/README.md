# Brand of the two editions

The product marks of the platform. The public edition shows no code name: its wordmark is its launcher name (ĐẦU TƯ CÔNG) and its product name is Hệ thống quản lý đầu tư công. The enterprise edition keeps its own identity (wordmark MẠCH VỐN, product name Phần mềm quản lý đầu tư xây dựng). The words are not kept in this folder: each edition manifest says them (`brand` of `packages/kernel/src/edition.ts` and of `packages/edition-enterprise/src/manifest.ts`), `build.mjs` reads them from there, and `node scripts/edition-brand.mjs` holds the phone and the static web files to them.

## The mark

One master shape for both editions: an **M built from rising bars** over a **gold base line**. A short pillar and a tall pillar stand on one line, a chevron between them is the valley of the M, and the base line is the foundation the capital stands on. The geometry lives in one place, `apps/web/src/pwa/icon-art.ts` (`MARK`); the PWA icons, the SVG sources here, the PNG exports and the phone launcher icons are all drawn from it, so they cannot drift apart.

| | Public edition | Enterprise edition |
|---|---|---|
| Product | Hệ thống quản lý đầu tư công | Phần mềm quản lý đầu tư xây dựng |
| Wordmark | ĐẦU TƯ CÔNG | MẠCH VỐN |
| Tagline | Vốn thông suốt, đầu tư công minh bạch | Vốn đến đúng công trình, việc đúng tiến độ |
| Tile gradient | `#1B4A5B` to `#0A2029` (flat `#102E3B` in PWA icons) | `#16507A` to `#061826` (flat `#0F3B57`) |
| Pillars | `#C4D5DC`, `#FFFFFF` | `#B9CFE0`, `#FFFFFF` |
| Chevron | `#2FB5A8` (teal) | `#27B7CF` (cyan) |
| Accent (base line, underline) | `#E0AA52` (gold) | `#F28C3A` (amber) |
| On a light page | pillars `#7EA2B1`, `#102E3B`; chevron `#007D77`; accent `#C98F2E` | pillars `#6E93B0`, `#0B2A44`; chevron `#0E8FA6`; accent `#D9701C` |

Typeface: Be Vietnam Pro (OFL, `apps/web/public/fonts/OFL.txt`). Wordmark in Bold, tracked +22% to +26%; product line in Medium; tagline in Regular. In the SVG files every word is an outline, so no font is needed to display them.

## Files

`brand/logo/*.svg` (sources), per edition `public` and `enterprise`:

- `logo-<edition>-icon.svg` rounded-square app icon (512 units, corner 112).
- `logo-<edition>-mark.svg`, `-mark-ondark.svg` the bare mark for a light or a dark page.
- `logo-<edition>-horizontal.svg`, `-horizontal-ondark.svg` icon at the left, wordmark, gold underline, product name.
- `logo-<edition>-stacked.svg`, `-stacked-ondark.svg` icon over the wordmark, underline, product name and tagline.
- `logo-<edition>-hero.svg` the film still: gradient, concentric rings, stacked lockup (1920 x 1080).

`node --disable-warning=ExperimentalWarning brand/build.mjs [--out D:\PIS-clips-out\brand]` rebuilds the SVG sources, writes the 2048 px transparent PNGs (3840 px for the hero) with Playwright, and writes `brand.json` (colours, file names, taglines) beside them. `brand/font-paths.mjs` is the small TrueType reader that turns text into outlines.

Derived assets, regenerated by their own scripts: the PWA icons (`npm run pwa:icons` in `apps/web`), the favicon of each edition and the phone launcher icons (`node apps/mobile/tool/generate_launcher_icons.mjs`).

## Usage rules

- **Clear space**: keep free, on every side, at least a quarter of the width of the icon tile. Nothing else enters this space.
- **Minimum size**: the icon 16 px (the favicon cut), the horizontal lockup 120 px wide for the icon alone plus text from 240 px, the stacked lockup 160 px wide; below that use the icon.
- **Backgrounds**: the tile works on light and on dark pages (the `ondark` files add a hairline so the tile does not melt into a dark page). Use the `ondark` text variants on dark photos or video, the plain ones on white and light grey.
- **Do not**: recolour the pillars, swap the accent between editions, add shadows or outlines, stretch, rotate, set the wordmark in another typeface, or place the mark on a busy photo without a dark scrim.
- **Editions**: the public edition shows the petrol and gold set, the enterprise edition the navy and amber set. Never mix the two sets in one view.
